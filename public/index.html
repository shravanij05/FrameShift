Questions

Will the quality change?

A little. Any conversion re-encodes the video, so it can't be pixel-identical. At the default setting it looks very close to the original. Two changes are deliberate and visible on a large screen: 4K is scaled down to 1080p, and HDR colours are converted to standard colours.

What's the difference between Balanced and Fast?

Both aim for the same picture quality. Fast finishes sooner but produces a larger file.

Is my video kept?

No. Uploaded and converted files are deleted from the server 20 minutes after upload.

Why does converting take a while?

Video encoding is heavy work, and long or 4K clips take longer. The page shows the time left once the conversion is under way.
